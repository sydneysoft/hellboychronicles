CORE LANGUAGE GUIDE

SPANISH GRAMMAR

A compact reference for the structures you meet most often in the Universal Core Stories.

Subject pronouns

yoI
túyou (informal singular)
él / ella / ustedhe / she / you formal
nosotros / nosotraswe
vosotros / vosotrasyou plural, mainly Spain
ellos / ellas / ustedesthey / you plural

Spanish often drops the subject pronoun because the verb ending already shows who is acting: “Tengo una llave” = “I have a key.”

Ser vs estar

seridentity, origin, profession, general characteristics · Misha es peruano.
estarlocation and temporary states · La llave está en la bolsa. Estoy cansado.

Articles & gender

el / losmasculine singular / plural
la / lasfeminine singular / plural
un / unosa/an, some — masculine
una / unasa/an, some — feminine

Learn the article together with each noun: la llave, el autobús, la ciudad.

Adjective agreement

cansadomasculine singular · Él está cansado.
cansadafeminine singular · Ella está cansada.
cansadosmasculine/mixed plural
cansadasfeminine plural
